| -------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `event-log-cleanup`        | hour                      | Removes expired event log entries and idempotency records.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | yes                           |
| `audit-cleanup`            | day                       | Removes expired audit entries and outbound webhook delivery history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | yes                           |
| `inbound-delivery-cleanup` | minute                    | Removes inbound deliveries after their handled or pending retention window.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | yes                           |
| `heartbeat`                | minute                    | Pings the liveness receiver `heartbeat_url` names.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | no                            |
| `version-thinning`         | hour                      | Thins item versions according to the configured retention policy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | yes                           |
| `feed-expiry`              | hour                      | Moves expired feed items to the trash.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | yes                           |
| `trash-purge`              | day                       | Permanently removes items whose trash retention has elapsed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | yes                           |
| `credential-cleanup`       | hour                      | Removes expired sign-in sessions, codes and tokens, retires application grants not used within the inactivity window, removes revoked grants and unused registered clients after their retention windows, removes revoked or expired keys after their retention window, and removes connector mapping previews that have expired.                                                       | yes                           |
| `blob-replicate`           | minute                    | Copies each blob into every attached store that lacks it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | no                            |
| `blob-integrity`           | hour                      | Checks blob copies and removes copies whose bytes are missing or altered.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | no                            |
| `blob-orphans`             | day                       | Reports unreferenced blobs and purges them after the grace period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | yes                           |
| `enrichment-sweep`         | 30 seconds                | Extracts searchable text and image dimensions from uploaded files.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | no                            |
| `attention-check`          | 5 minutes                 | Checks what needs the owner's attention and announces each new entry (`events/attention-raised`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | no                            |
| `bulk-action-gc`           | hour                      | Removes finished bulk action jobs after 7 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | yes                           |
| `table-row-cleanup`        | hour                      | Removes the rows and change logs of purged items, the batches of a table's change log older than `row_log_retention_days`, rewrites the order a table keeps after its `order` changed and the search index of its rows after what it marks searchable changed, and writes or removes the order the server keeps of a large table's rows by a column it was sorted by, a page at a time. | yes                           |
| `release-check`            | 6 hours                   | Asks GitHub for the newest published Marfa release and keeps what it finds (`updates.md`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | no                            |
| `backup`                   | `backup_interval_seconds` | Puts a backup of the instance in the backup folder or the object store when anything it holds has changed, and thins this instance's backups.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | no                            |
