---
title: What the CLI, connectors and apps send
description: See what the CLI and connectors report about themselves, when, how to turn it off, and what to expect of an app.
---

The CLI, connectors and apps report on themselves apart from the instance. **None sends anything until it's given a destination**: the CLI and a connector by the environment they run in, and an app by the people who build it. What an instance sends is in [Send an instance's telemetry](/run/telemetry).

## What the CLI sends

**The CLI sends one trace for each command, and only when its environment names an OpenTelemetry collector** in `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` or `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TRACES_ENDPOINT`. With neither set, it sends nothing, and `OTEL_SDK_DISABLED=true` turns the trace off. It sends nothing to PostHog.

The trace is named for the command, such as `marfa items create`, and carries its exit code, the core's operations beneath it with their counts and outcomes, and the error code of a refusal. **It holds no argument, address, credential, item content or message,** and names no person. A collector that is unreachable or slow changes neither the command's output nor its exit code, and the command waits for it no longer than its timeout, two seconds by default.

A folder watch that [starts when you log in](/use/keep-folders-syncing#start-the-watch-when-you-log-in) carries the `OTEL_*` settings set in the shell that turned it on. [The CLI's telemetry reference](https://github.com/withmarfa/marfa/blob/main/core/marfa-cli/COMMANDS.md#telemetry) lists every setting it reads.

## What connectors send

**A connector built on the connector kit sends nothing of its own** unless its environment sets `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`. Then it exports its traces and logs over OTLP to that address, and nowhere else.

A failed run reaches you through your instance, not through telemetry: the connector reports the run with its error, and `marfa attention` lists a connector whose last run failed. A start that fails before the connector registers is only logged, and exported over OTLP when that is on. [Make a connector follow changes](/build/connector-follow-changes#read-a-failed-run) shows how to read a failed run.

## What apps send

**What an app sends is decided by the people who build it,** and its own settings say how to turn it off. **The Swift SDK sends nothing itself** and holds no reporting code. An app that reports its errors with a tool of its own can send each error's [fixed text](/reference/swift-sdk/errors-and-testing#handle-errors), which holds no item content, credential or address.
