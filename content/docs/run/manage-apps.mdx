---
title: Manage apps
description: Approve what an app may reach, give it less than it asks for, and narrow or disconnect it later.
---

An app, such as Claude, a reading app or the `marfa` command line, signs in to your instance and asks for access. **It reaches only what the owner approves,** and keeps it until the owner disconnects it or it goes unused for a year. A script you run yourself uses a [key](/run/manage-keys) instead. [Access](/build/access) explains what a credential can hold.

## Approve an app

An app that signs in opens an approval page in your browser, headed **Allow** and the app's name. An app with no browser of its own, such as the CLI on a server, shows a code instead: open the address it gives, enter the code on the **Enter your code** page within ten minutes, and check that the approval page shows the same code. Sign in as the owner if the page asks.

**Marfa can't confirm who made an app,** because any program can register under any name. The page says so and names where the app sends you back. Approve only apps you trust, and never a link or a code someone sent you.

1. Read **What to allow**, which lists everything the app asked for: what it may read only, what it may read and edit, and the control over Marfa it wants. A last line, **Can't be undone**, names any of `instance.maintain`, `schema.write`, `items.purge`, `backups.manage`, `config.manage` and `connectors.manage`.
2. Choose **Full access** to allow all of it, or **Choose access** to pick:
   - **Already allowed:** what the app held before. Approving never lowers it.
   - **Your data:** **No access**, **Read only** or **Read and edit** for each part, up to what the app asked. An app that asks for **All your data** can be given chosen types instead, with **Choose types**.
   - **Your profile:** shown only when the app asks for it.
   - **Look after this Marfa:** each [permission](/catalog/permissions) starts off until you turn it on.
3. Select **Approve**, or **Deny** to decline.

**Approving with everything new turned off is declining:** the app gets `access_denied` and keeps what it held. An app that comes back for more shows only what's new, beside what it already holds.

Granting a write or a permission the app doesn't hold needs a sign-in from the last five minutes, so the page asks you to [confirm it's you](/run/sign-in-as-the-owner#confirm-its-you) and then approves.

## Narrow or disconnect an app

Name the scopes the app keeps: the list you give is the whole of what it holds afterwards.

```bash
marfa apps narrow <id> --scope offline_access --scope core.file.image:read
```

- **Narrowing ends at once** every token and refresh chain that holds more than what's left, and the rest keep working. An app whose sign-in ended signs in again with the access left, and asking for more brings back the approval page. `--clear` removes every scope.
- **A narrowing never widens:** a list that would give more than the app holds is refused `403 forbidden`. If the app's access changed since the CLI read it, the change is refused `409 conflict` and nothing changes.

To disconnect an app:

```bash
marfa apps revoke <id>
```

Its tokens are refused `401 unauthorized` at once, and its next sign-in shows the approval page again. **The keys the app made keep working** unless you add `--revoke-keys`, which revokes them and every key they made, in the same step. An unknown ID answers `404 grant_not_found`.

Narrowing leaves the keys the app made as they were, so narrow those as [Manage keys](/run/manage-keys#narrow-a-key) shows. An app disconnects itself as [Authenticate an app](/build/authenticate) shows, which also leaves its keys working.

## When an app's access ends by itself

Two [stored settings](/run/settings#change-settings-while-it-runs) clear apps nobody uses:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grant_inactivity_days` | `365` | An approval ends once the app goes this many days unused. The keys it made keep working. `0` keeps every approval. |
| `unapproved_app_retention_hours` | `1` | An app that registered itself and was never approved is removed after this many hours. `0` keeps it. |

Moving the instance's [sign-in address](/run/address#move-the-address) signs every app out, and each signs in again at the new address with the access it held.

The rules behind this page are in the [keys and sign-in](https://github.com/withmarfa/marfa/blob/main/conformance/spec/keys-and-oauth.md) chapter of the contract.
