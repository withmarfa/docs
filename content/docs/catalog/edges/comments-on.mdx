A comment is a **part** of the item it's on, its **host**. It goes to the bin with its host, and so do the replies to it at every depth. They come back when the host does, unless you binned them on their own, and are purged with it. Removing the edge leaves both items where they are.

A chain of `comments-on` and `attached-to` edges can't loop back to where it started: the edge that would close it is refused `400 edge_cycle`.

## Example

A reply to a comment:

```json
{
  "edge_type": "comments-on",
  "from_id": "01a1f139-e03c-735c-9ddf-83a546ebebb5",
  "to_id": "01a1f139-e038-76f6-a27c-0508bfc3ed4d"
}
```
