<include cwd>src/generated/catalog/edges.md#parent-of</include>

- **One parent.** A child has at most one parent. A second is refused `400 edge_constraint_violation`.
- **No loops.** An edge that would make an item its own ancestor is refused `400 edge_cycle`.
- **In the bin,** a parent takes its children with it at every depth, even those the key can't read. Restoring the parent brings back the children its trip took, and leaves any child you binned before it. Binning a child leaves its parent.

## Example

A task, `from_id`, made the parent of another, `to_id`:

```json
{
  "edge_type": "parent-of",
  "from_id": "01a1f139-e055-774a-a8a5-b20cdad1b4a3",
  "to_id": "01a1f139-e05b-7246-94b7-d9ff13926d57"
}
```
