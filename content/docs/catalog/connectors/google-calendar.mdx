It never invites anyone, and every write tells Google to email nobody.

## What it brings

It reads calendars on the account's calendar list that the account owns or can write, including hidden ones, and the events on them. It skips the Birthdays calendar. A calendar the account can only read is skipped unless its ID is named in `google_calendar_read_only_calendars`, separated from any others by commas. A read-only calendar you stop naming, or one whose access drops to read only, is archived with its events.

Past and canceled events are read, because Google reports a deletion as a canceled event, and that row is then archived. Events of the kind `workingLocation` are skipped. Other reserved kinds, such as out of office, are read and marked so Marfa does not change them.

Each event connects to its calendar with `google.calendar.in-calendar`. Trashing the calendar in Marfa leaves the events. A recurring event is one row whose `recurrence` holds the rules, and Marfa expands the occurrences. A moved occurrence is its own row, under the series. Moving an event between two calendars of the account keeps the row when Google says the event has left the first calendar.

## What goes back

Changes to an event go back: title, description, place, status, times, the rules of a series, a move to another calendar, and trash, restore and purge. A new event is made on the calendar the row connects to, or on the primary calendar when it connects to none. `status` is `confirmed` or `tentative`. Trash the row to delete the event.

Marfa does not change an event it marks not editable. That includes a calendar the account can only read, a reserved kind, a locked event, an event someone else organizes, and an event with guests other than the account. A change to one of those is put back, and a trash leaves the event in Google.

A batch that would delete more than 10 events of one calendar, and more than a quarter of the events that calendar holds, is held, and the run stops: nothing is read or carried to Google until you restore the trashed rows, or set `google_calendar_confirm_deletes` to the value the run names, for that one batch, and then clear it.

## Sign-in and settings

Sign in with `marfa plugins sign-in google-calendar`, as [Give a plugin's connector access to your Google account](/use/connector-google-access) describes. The grant needs `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events` and read-only access to the calendar list. You set `google_client_id` with `marfa plugins settings` and `google_client_secret` with `marfa plugins secret` ([Give a connector its settings and secrets](/use/connector-settings)). Marfa stores the refresh token as the secret `google_refresh_token`. Until you have signed in, a run stops.

`google_calendar_read_only_calendars` names read-only calendars to read. The plugin's `plugin.json` lists every setting.

One instance supports one Google account. A grant for another fails the run.

## Install

Install the plugin from its source folder, as [Install a plugin](/use/install-a-plugin) describes. Its connector runs on a connector host ([Run a plugin's connector](/use/run-a-plugin-connector)).
