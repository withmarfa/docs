The Readwise Reader connector keeps the documents, highlights and annotations of a Reader library in the feed, along with a copy of their content, and carries supported edits back to Reader. The [Readwise connector](/catalog/connectors/readwise) works without it.

## What it reads

- **Documents** in your saved library. Feed documents are left out unless you set `READWISE_READER_INCLUDE_FEED`.
- **Highlights and annotations** on those documents. Reader supplies the canonical passage. A note record is folded into the annotation. A highlight's `body` is the export passage as Markdown, which keeps pictures and formatting. It is Reader's whole passage when there is no export passage or Readwise cut it at 8,191 characters. Its `title` is the first paragraph of the export passage, which keeps the paragraph breaks Reader's passage loses, or of Reader's passage when the export has no words. A heading in either passage ends the title, and a passage with only a picture is titled after its Reader document. Dates, times and addresses follow the [Readwise connector's](/catalog/connectors/readwise#types-it-writes) field kinds.
- **Content.** A copy of each document's HTML, its original file where there is one, Readwise-hosted images, text too long for a field, and a manifest of what was kept, pending or unavailable. Each is a `readwise.file` row. Content downloads come only from Readwise's asset storage host, and no API credentials go to it.
- **Pictures from other websites,** only if you set `READWISE_READER_PICTURES`. By default the manifest lists their addresses and nothing is fetched ([Saving pictures](#saving-pictures)).

A listing walk starts from the last completed walk with a five-minute overlap, and a full reconciliation repeats daily.

## Types it writes

| Type | Parent type | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `readwise.source` | [Media](/catalog/media) | A Reader document |
| `readwise.highlight` | [Highlight](/catalog/highlight) | A highlight with its annotation and tags |
| `readwise.file` | [File](/catalog/file) | Kept content, attached to its document or highlight |

The Readwise connector writes the same types, so one row can hold what both connectors know. A row Reader manages carries a Reader link, `<account>:reader-document:<ID>` or `<account>:reader-highlight:<ID>`, in `canonical_key`. A highlight connects to its document through `references`, and a file connects to what it belongs to through `attached-to`.

## What goes back

- **Document edits.** The title, author, summary (`description`), notes, tags, reading location (`reader_location`) and supported categories.
- **Highlight edits.** The annotation (`note`) and tags. An empty note or tag list clears them.
- **A new row.** Set `reader_create: true` and your `account` label on a `readwise.source` that has a stable HTTP URL, or on a `readwise.highlight` that has `reader_parent_id` and passage text. A row without that flag is never created in Reader.

Only changed supported fields are sent. Body replacement, passage edits, a highlight's `title` and `body`, reading progress and original-file upload stay read only. Trashing a row changes nothing in Reader.

## Saving pictures

A document's HTML often shows pictures from other websites. `READWISE_READER_PICTURES` decides what happens to them. Pictures on Readwise's own storage are kept whatever it says.

- **`addresses`:** the manifest lists each picture from another website as not kept, with the reason `third-party`, and nothing is fetched.
- **`save`:** the connector fetches and saves the pictures of every document.
- **`save-in-library`:** it saves the pictures only of documents whose `readwise.source` row is in the [library](/items-and-types#library-and-feed). A document in the feed is treated as with `addresses`.

**The HTML is not changed:** it keeps the addresses the document gave. A picture is the address in an `img` element's `src`, or in its `data-src` when it has no `src`, or the document's cover `image_url`. Each whole address is one picture, so two addresses that differ only in the query are two pictures. Other image references, such as `srcset` candidates and relative or `data:` addresses, stay listed with the reason `third-party` and are never fetched.

Each saved picture is a `readwise.file` row with `resource_kind` `picture`, the picture's bytes and `mime_type`, and `source_url`, the address it was saved from. An `attached-to` connection runs from the picture to its document, and the picture takes the document's tier when it is created.

**Pictures never hold up a document's content.** The connector keeps a document's HTML and other content first, and its manifest is complete without the pictures. Saving pictures is separate work, with its own queue and limits, that runs after the listing and the content in each scheduled run. It reads the document from Reader once, saves its pictures, and writes the manifest again at the same revision. A run that a webhook started, or that Reader asks to slow down, leaves pictures to the next run.

The manifest lists each picture with what became of it:

- **Kept:** Marfa holds the picture, with its bytes and address, and its `attached-to` connection to the document. This holds whichever connector wrote the manifest and whatever the setting, so a manifest the [Readwise](/catalog/connectors/readwise) connector writes lists the pictures this one saved. A saved picture that lacks its connection, as a run that stopped early can leave one, gets it again and is kept from the next run. If the connection still hasn't landed after 5 such repairs in a row, the connector stops, lists the picture as not kept with the reason `third-party`, and raises a condition whose key is `readwise:pictures-repair:` followed by a hash of the document's canonical key.
- **Not kept,** with a reason. `third-party` is a picture not saved yet, or not saved under this setting. `not_https` is an `http:` address. `over-document-limit` is a picture past the first 50 of a document. `removed` is a picture a person moved to the bin, archived or purged, and it is never fetched again.
- **Unavailable,** with the reason the fetch gave and the HTTP status where there is one. The fetch refuses a private address, a known tracker's counting pixel, an SVG, a one-pixel image, and a response that is not a picture or is too large. A refused picture is not fetched again: the connector remembers the 1,024 most recent refusals across runs. A timeout, a failed connection, a failed upload, or an HTTP 408, 425, 429 or 5xx is tried again in later runs, 3 times in all, and is then unavailable.

One failed picture never fails its document or the run.

| Limit | Value | Past it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pictures saved from one document | 50 | The rest are not kept, with the reason `over-document-limit` |
| Pictures fetched in one run | 100 | The rest wait for the next run |
| Bytes saved in one run | 200 MiB | The rest wait, from the picture that would pass it |
| Time spent fetching pictures | 10 minutes from the run's first picture fetch | The rest wait |
| Documents read for pictures in one run | 25 | The rest wait |

A run that reaches one of the limits raises the condition `readwise:reader-pictures-limit`, and the next run goes on. At most 256 documents wait for pictures; past that, the connector raises `readwise:reader-pictures-backlog` and later goes over the documents it keeps again.

**A document is tried again, never dropped.** When a document's picture work fails, its content isn't kept yet, or a picture needed its connection repaired, the connector tries the document again in a later run, after a wait that doubles from 2 minutes up to 24 hours, as it does for content. If the connector's saved picture progress can't be read, it raises `readwise:reader-pictures-invalid`, starts again from an empty queue, and goes over the documents it keeps again, and the listing goes on. With the setting at `addresses`, it clears that progress.

**Turning the setting on reaches documents already kept.** The connector doesn't walk the Reader listing again. It goes over the documents it keeps in Marfa a batch at a time, with one Reader request for each document, so each gets its pictures over later runs. It uploads nothing already kept, and changes no existing row except to add pictures and update the manifest. Turning the setting off stops this work, and turning it on again starts it over.

## Settings

| Setting | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `READWISE_ACCESS_TOKEN` | Your Readwise access token. A secret. |
| `READWISE_ACCOUNT` | The same stable account label as the Readwise connector. |
| `READWISE_READER_INCLUDE_FEED` | `true` includes feed documents. The default is `false`. |
| `READWISE_READER_INBOUND_ONLY` | `true` turns off every edit going back. The default is `false`. |
| `READWISE_READER_PROPAGATE_PURGE` | `true` lets a permanent purge in Marfa delete the Reader record. The default is `false`. |
| `READWISE_CONTENT_MAX_BYTES` | The largest resource to keep, in bytes, up to 1,073,741,824. The default is 268,435,456 (256 MiB). |
| `READWISE_READER_PICTURES` | What to do with pictures on other websites: `addresses` (the default), `save` or `save-in-library`. Any other value stops the connector at start. |

## What to know

- **Give Reader its own key source.** The key's own source is `readwise-reader` and it also claims `readwise`, which you set through the instance's private socket with `--claim readwise`. The Readwise connector's key uses the source `readwise`, so the two keep separate progress.
- **Use one account label in both connectors.** Never change it against saved progress.
- **Purges are capped.** With `READWISE_READER_PROPAGATE_PURGE` on, a run that would delete more than 20 Reader records fails before it reads or sends anything. It keeps failing until the count is 20 or fewer or you turn propagation off.
- **A disappearance needs proof.** A failed request or an incomplete listing never archives a row. More than 20 confirmed disappearances archive none.
- **Over-limit content stays pending.** A resource larger than `READWISE_CONTENT_MAX_BYTES` keeps its last good copy and reports the limit.
- **An existing Readwise install needs a schema step first.** Replace the two type definitions as the [Readwise README](https://github.com/withmarfa/marfa-connectors/blob/main/connectors/readwise/README.md#replace-existing-schemas) describes, then restart both connectors. A change to a field's kind needs the same step, as [Upgrade to highlight titles and field kinds](https://github.com/withmarfa/marfa-connectors/blob/main/connectors/readwise/README.md#upgrade-to-highlight-titles-and-field-kinds) describes.

## Run it

[Run a connector](/run/run-a-connector) runs any connector. This one's key holds write on `readwise.source`, `readwise.highlight` and `readwise.file`, and on the `references` and `attached-to` connections, and its [README](https://github.com/withmarfa/marfa-connectors/blob/main/connectors/readwise-reader/README.md) lists what the key needs. Saving pictures needs no other permission.
