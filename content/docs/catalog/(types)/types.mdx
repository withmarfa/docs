Marfa ships two families of types: **core types** for what people keep, such as notes and tasks, and **system types** for Marfa's own records, which it writes itself. [Type rules](/catalog/your-own-types) and [Field kinds](/catalog/field-kinds) list what a type you register must meet and what a field can hold.

[Items and types](/items-and-types) explains how types work, and [Define a type](/build/define-a-type) shows how to add your own.
