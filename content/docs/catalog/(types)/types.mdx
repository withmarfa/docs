Marfa ships **core types** for what you keep, such as files, notes, and bookmarks, and **system types** for records it writes itself. Each type's page lists its fields. [Type definition](/catalog/type-definition) and [Field kinds](/catalog/field-kinds) say what a type must meet.

Types under `user.*` are your own and are not listed here, and families under `common.*` come from Marfa plugins, such as [Tickets](/catalog/tickets).

[Items and types](/build/items-and-types) explains how types work.
