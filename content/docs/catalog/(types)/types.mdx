Marfa ships two families of types: **core types** for what people keep, such as notes and tasks, and **system types** for Marfa's own records, which it writes itself. Each type's page lists its fields and any subtypes. [Type rules](/catalog/type-rules), [Display hints](/catalog/display-hints) and [Field kinds](/catalog/field-kinds) list what a type you register must meet, how its items are shown and what a field can hold.

[Items and types](/items-and-types) explains how types work, and [Define a type](/build/define-a-type) shows how to add your own.
