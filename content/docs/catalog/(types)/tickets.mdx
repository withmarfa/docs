Tickets is a [plugin](/use/install-a-plugin) WithMarfa publishes in [withmarfa/plugins](https://github.com/withmarfa/plugins), with the mark `lucide:ticket`. It needs Marfa 0.0.92 or later.

```bash
marfa plugins install tickets
```

## Types

Each type comes with guidance an agent reads before it writes one. Write a ticket as one of the four kinds, never as a plain `common.ticket`.

| Type | Mark | Parent | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `common.ticket` | `lucide:ticket` | [`core.task`](/catalog/task) | Groups the kinds: work someone wants done, or a question they want answered. Ask for it to read every ticket at once. |
| `common.ticket.epic` | `lucide:flag` | `common.ticket` | An outcome that needs several tickets, `completed` when it's reached. |
| `common.ticket.feature` | `lucide:sparkles` | `common.ticket` | A change people using a product or service would notice. |
| `common.ticket.spike` | `lucide:flask-conical` | `common.ticket` | A question to answer before deciding or building. `description` holds the question and `body` the answer. |
| `common.ticket.task` | `lucide:wrench` | `common.ticket` | Other work, such as upkeep, setup, cleanup or a fix. |

## Fields

A ticket has the fields of [Task](/catalog/task#fields) and adds none: `title`, `description` (the line a board shows), `body`, `status`, `priority`, `assignees`, `due_at`, `starts_at`, `completed_at`, `place`, `precision`, `url`, `notes` and `recurrence`.

`status` takes one word more than a task's:

| Word | Group | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `backlog` | `not_started` | Wanted, but not yet committed to. |

## Edges

| Edge type | From | To | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`parent-of`](/catalog/edges/parent-of) | An epic | Each of its tickets | Read from a ticket as `child-of`. A ticket has at most one parent, and moving an epic to the bin moves its tickets too. |
| [`derived-from`](/catalog/edges/derived-from) | A ticket | What it was made from | Such as a note, a message, or the spike that led to it. |
