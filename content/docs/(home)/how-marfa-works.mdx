Marfa is **one home for everything you keep**: your files first, then the notes, tasks and bookmarks around them. Take a trip to Lisbon: a beach photo, a packing list, and a task to book flights.

## Items

- **An item is one thing you keep:** the photo, the packing list, the task. Each keeps its own history of versions.
- **A file you save becomes an item.** Marfa keeps the photo's bytes, and the item holds its title, its tags and any text Marfa reads from it.
- **An item's type says what kind of thing it is,** such as a note, a task or a photo, and which details it holds, such as a due date. Types nest: a photo is a kind of file, so a search for files finds your photos too.
- **Every app and agent you approve works on the same items.**

## How items connect

- **A tag is a free label,** such as `lisbon`, on any item of any type.
- **An edge links one item to another and says how:** the photo is **attached to** the packing list. The photo stays an item of its own.
- **A view is a saved list,** such as every item tagged `lisbon`, newest first. It's an item too, so every app shows the same list.

## Where items sit

- **The library holds what you chose to keep:** what you saved, or what an agent saved for you.
- **The feed holds what services send** through connectors, such as your Todoist tasks. **Keeping an item moves it to the library;** it stays the same item. An item you don't keep stays in the feed, unless its type sets how many days the feed holds it.
- **The bin holds what you delete,** with its attached files. You can restore an item as it was for 60 days, unless you [change that setting](/run/settings).
- **A synced folder puts items on your computer as files.** The folder lives on your instance; the directory, such as `~/Documents/Lisbon`, is where its files sit. The photo is `beach.png`, the note `Packing list.md`, and a change on either side reaches the other. See [Sync a folder](/use/sync-a-folder).

## Who reaches your items

- **You're the owner,** the one person in your instance, and you sign in with your password or a passkey.
- **An app or agent** signs in through your browser, and you approve what it may reach: which types, and whether it may change them.
- **A key** is a credential you make for a script or a connector, holding only what you name.

You can [see and cut off](/use/see-and-cut-access) any app or key at any time. The [Glossary](/glossary) defines every word these docs use.
