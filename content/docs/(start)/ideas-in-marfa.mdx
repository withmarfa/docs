---
title: Ideas in Marfa
description: The few ideas you meet everywhere in Marfa, in plain words.
---

Everything you keep in Marfa is an **item**, and a handful of ideas say what an item is, how it connects to others and who can reach it. They are the same whether you work from an agent, the CLI or a folder of files.

## Items

An item is **one thing you keep**: a photo, a PDF, a note, a bookmark, a task, a person, a trip. Each has its own ID, its own history of versions, and the time it happened, such as when a photo was taken or a message was sent.

Every app and agent you approve works on the same items. When Claude saves a bookmark, the CLI finds it, and so does any app you use next.

## Types

An item's **type** says what kind of thing it is and what it holds. A `core.bookmark` holds a title and an address, a `core.task` holds a status and a due date, and a `core.file.image` holds a picture with its size and format.

Marfa ships types for the everyday kinds of things people keep, listed in the [Catalog](/catalog/types). Types can be more specific: a photo is a `core.file.image`, which is a kind of `core.file`, so a search for files finds it too. **You can add your own types,** such as a recipe or a wine you tasted, and an agent can draft one for you.

Types are also how access works. You let an app reach notes but not files, or read bookmarks but not change them, by type.

## Files

A file you save, such as a PDF, a photo or a voice memo, becomes **an item whose type is `core.file` or a kind of it**. Marfa keeps the file's bytes, and the item holds its title, tags and details, so a file is found, tagged and connected like anything else.

A note can show a picture, and a task can carry a document. The file stays an item of its own, attached to the item it belongs to.

## Tags

A **tag** is a free label you put on any item, such as `lisbon` or `taxes-2026`. Tags are flat: no tag sits inside another. An item can carry several, and a search can ask for every item that carries a tag, whatever its type.

## Edges

An **edge** links one item to another, and says how: a note **references** a bookmark, a file is **attached to** a note, a task is the **parent of** its subtasks. An edge has a direction, from one item to the other.

Edges let you follow your data around: from a trip to its tickets, from a person to the messages they sent. The [Catalog](/catalog/edges) lists the kinds of edges Marfa ships.

## The library and the feed

Every item sits in one of two **tiers**:

- **The library** holds what you chose to keep: what you saved yourself, or what an agent saved for you.
- **The feed** holds what arrives in volume from other services, such as your Todoist tasks or the posts in an RSS feed, as each service sent it.

**Moving an item to the library is how you keep it.** The item stays the same item; only its tier changes. A connector writes to the feed, so a busy service never crowds out what you chose.

## Tables

Some items hold **a table**: rows in named columns, such as the lines of a budget or the messages of a conversation. A row is part of its item, not an item of its own, so a long conversation is one item with many rows.

## The bin

When you delete an item, it **moves to the bin**. You can restore it as it was until it's deleted for good, 60 days later unless you [change that setting](/run/settings). What belongs to the item, such as a file attached to it, moves with it.

## Who reaches your items

You're the **owner** of your instance, and the only person in it. Everything else reaches it with a credential you gave it:

- **An app or agent** signs in through your browser, and you approve what it may reach: which types, and whether it may change them.
- **A key** is a credential you make for a script, a connector or an agent that runs on its own, holding only what you name.

You can [see and cut off](/use/see-and-cut-access) any app or key at any time. [Access](/build/access) explains the rules in full, and the [Glossary](/glossary) defines every word these docs use.
