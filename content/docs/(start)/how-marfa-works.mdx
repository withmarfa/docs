Marfa is **one home for everything you keep**: your files first, then the notes, tasks, bookmarks and messages around them. The same ideas hold whether you work from an agent, the CLI or a folder of files.

<MarfaModel />

## What you keep

### Items

- **An item is one thing you keep:** a photo, a PDF, a note, a task, a person, a trip.
- Each has its own history of versions, and the time it happened, such as when a photo was taken.
- **Every app and agent you approve works on the same items,** so a PDF Claude saves is one the CLI finds.

[Items and types](/build/items-and-types) has the details.

### Types

- **An item's type says what kind of thing it is** and which details it holds, such as a title or a due date.
- Types nest: a photo is a kind of file, so a search for files finds your photos too.
- Marfa ships types for everyday things, listed in the [Catalog](/catalog/types), and **you can add your own,** such as a recipe.

### Files

- **A file you save becomes an item** of a file type. Marfa keeps its bytes, and the item holds its title, tags and the text Marfa reads from it.
- A file can be attached to a note or a task, and stays an item of its own.

[Files](/build/files) explains how a file is stored once.

## How items connect

### Tags

- **A tag is a free label,** such as `lisbon` or `taxes-2026`, on any item of any type.
- Tags are flat: no tag sits inside another.

### Edges

- **An edge links one item to another, and says how:** a photo is **attached to** a note, a note **references** a bookmark, a task is the **parent of** its subtasks.
- An edge points one way, so you can follow a trip to its tickets, or a person to their messages.

[Edges](/build/edges) lists the kinds and when each fits.

### Views

- **A view is a saved list of items,** such as every item tagged `lisbon`, newest first.
- It's an item too, so every app you use shows the same list.

## Where items sit

### The library and the feed

- **The library holds what you chose to keep:** what you saved, or what an agent saved for you.
- **The feed holds what arrives in volume** from services through connectors, such as your Todoist tasks, as each service sent it.
- **Moving an item to the library is how you keep it.** It stays the same item; only its place changes.

### The bin

- **A deleted item moves to the bin,** with what belongs to it, such as its attached files.
- You can restore it as it was until it expires, 60 days later unless you [change that setting](/run/settings).

## Who reaches your items

- **You're the owner,** the one person in your instance, and you sign in with your password or a passkey.
- **An app or agent** signs in through your browser, and you approve what it may reach: which types, and whether it may change them.
- **A key** is a credential you make for a script or a connector, holding only what you name.

You can [see and cut off](/use/see-and-cut-access) any app or key at any time. [Access](/build/access) has the rules, and the [Glossary](/glossary) defines every word these docs use.
